# OpenAI

AI safety company and research lab founded in 2015. Creator of the GPT model
series, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Whisper, and Codex. Known for pioneering [[RLHF]] as a
practical alignment technique.

## Key Facts
- Founded: 2015 by Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Andrej Karpathy, Ilya Sutskever, and others
- Became a "capped-profit" company with Microsoft investment (2019)
- Released ChatGPT: November 2022 — fastest consumer product to 100M users
- Published InstructGPT (2022): foundational RLHF paper
- Models: GPT-3, GPT-3.5, GPT-4, o1, o3

## Significance
OpenAI's InstructGPT paper established the three-stage training pipeline —
pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, and RLHF — that all major LLMs now follow.
Karpathy uses OpenAI's models throughout his lectures as reference implementations.
