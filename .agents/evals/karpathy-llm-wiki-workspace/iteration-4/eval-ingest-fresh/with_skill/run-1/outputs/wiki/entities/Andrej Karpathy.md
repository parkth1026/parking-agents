# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; founding member of OpenAI, later Director of AI at
Tesla, and co-creator of the popular "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" video
series. One of the most effective explainers of how large language models
actually work.

## Key Facts

- Founding member of OpenAI; previously led computer vision at Tesla
  (Autopilot).
- Author of widely watched educational talks and courses, including the
  general-audience talk [[Intro to Large Language Models]] (Nov 2023) that
  seeded this wiki's core concept pages.
- Known for framing an LLM as "just two files" (parameters + code) and for
  the [[LLM OS]] metaphor of models as the kernel of an emerging operating
  system.
- Strong advocate of open source and of building intuition first — explaining
  [[Next Token Prediction]] and [[Pretraining]] without heavy math.

## Significance

Karpathy's explanations are a common entry point into LLM fundamentals. His
mental models — the two-file LLM, pretraining vs fine-tuning as distinct
stages, the LLM-as-OS diagram — have shaped how the field talks about
[[Large Language Model]]s in plain language. This wiki follows that
style: intuition first, links everywhere.
