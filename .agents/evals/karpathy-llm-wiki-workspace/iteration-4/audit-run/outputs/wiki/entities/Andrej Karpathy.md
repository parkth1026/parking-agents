# Andrej Karpathy

Former Director of AI at Tesla, OpenAI founding member, and influential educator on deep learning.

## Key Facts
- Known for from-first-principles educational content (neural network videos, LLM talks)
- Delivered the [[Karpathy Intro to LLMs Talk]] that seeded this wiki

## Significance
Frames LLMs accessibly as next-token prediction systems trained in stages, and popularized the [[LLM OS]] analogy; his mental model of [[Next-Token Prediction]] underlies several pages in this wiki.
