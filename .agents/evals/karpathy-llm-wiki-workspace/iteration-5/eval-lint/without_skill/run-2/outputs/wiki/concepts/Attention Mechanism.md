# Attention Mechanism

Mechanism that computes a weighted combination of values, with weights derived from query–key similarity, letting a model focus on the most relevant parts of its input.

## Role
The core mechanism of the [[Transformer]] — introduced in "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017); see [[Big Source]] for survey context.
