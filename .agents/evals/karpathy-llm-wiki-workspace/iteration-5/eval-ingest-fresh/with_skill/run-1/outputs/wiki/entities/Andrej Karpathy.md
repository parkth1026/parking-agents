# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Founding member of OpenAI, later Director of AI at
Tesla (Autopilot vision), and a co-founder of xAI. Widely known for
exceptionally clear educational material on neural networks and language
models.

## Key Facts

- Founding member of OpenAI (2015); returned as a researcher there after the
  Tesla years (2023).
- Led the computer-vision side of Tesla Autopilot (2016-2022).
- Author of the course "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero", which builds GPT-style
  models from scratch.
- Gave the talk [[Intro to Large Language Models]] (2023) — the founding
  source of this wiki.

## Significance

One of the most effective explainers of LLM technology to a broad engineering
audience. His talks compress the full stack — the training objective,
pretraining vs fine-tuning, tooling, security — into compact mental models:
an LLM as "next token prediction on internet text", as a "parameters file plus
run file", and the ecosystem as a future [[LLM OS]].
