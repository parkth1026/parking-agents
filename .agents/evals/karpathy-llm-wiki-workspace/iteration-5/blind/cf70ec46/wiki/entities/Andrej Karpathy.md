# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator, known both for production-scale deep learning
work and for unusually clear public teaching material on neural networks and
language models.

## Key Facts

- Founding member of OpenAI (2015); later Director of AI at Tesla
  (Autopilot vision, 2017-2022); returned to OpenAI briefly (2023-2024).
- Co-created Stanford's CS231n (convolutional networks) and the "Neural
  Networks: Zero to Hero" YouTube series; maintains small educational code
  bases such as nanoGPT and micrograd.
- Author of [[Intro to Large Language Models (talk)]] (2023) — the first
  source ingested into this wiki.
- Popularized compact framings of the field, e.g., describing a
  [[Large Language Model]] as "just two files" (parameters plus a small
  runner).

## Significance

Karpathy is one of the most effective translators between LLM research and
practitioners. His introductory talks compress the full LLM pipeline —
[[Pretraining]], [[Fine-Tuning]], tooling, safety, and the [[LLM OS]] vision —
into mental models that non-specialists can retain, which is why his material
is a common seed for knowledge bases like this one.
