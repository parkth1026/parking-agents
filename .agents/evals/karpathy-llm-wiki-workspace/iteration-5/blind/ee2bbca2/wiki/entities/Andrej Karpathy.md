# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; founding member of OpenAI, later Senior Director of
AI at Tesla (Autopilot vision), then returned to OpenAI, and since then an
independent educator. Known for explaining deep learning "from scratch" —
building systems up from first principles in public.

## Key Facts

- Founding member of OpenAI (2015); led Tesla Autopilot's computer vision
  (2017-2022); rejoined OpenAI in 2023.
- Creator of widely used teaching materials: Stanford CS231n (convolutional
  networks), the "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" video series, and small
  reference implementations such as nanoGPT and micrograd.
- Gave the talk [[Intro to Large Language Models]] (2023), a common on-ramp
  into the field, which coined/popularized the [[LLM OS]] framing for this wiki.

## Significance

Karpathy is the field's best-known translator between research and practice.
His "build it yourself" approach (tiny, readable implementations of core ideas)
shaped how a generation of engineers learns [[Large Language Model]]
fundamentals, and his talks establish shared vocabulary — pretraining vs
fine-tuning, hallucination as a property of next-token prediction, the LLM OS
metaphor — that the rest of the literature now assumes.
