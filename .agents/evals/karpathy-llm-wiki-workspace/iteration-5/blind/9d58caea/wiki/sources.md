## Primary source for this wiki

- **Andrej Karpathy — *Intro to Large Language Models* (2023).** The talk this wiki was seeded from. Two versions exist:
  - Short version (~20 min), Oct 2023.
  - Extended version (~1 hr), Nov 2023 — includes the material on tool use, security, and the LLM OS in more depth.
  - Both are on Karpathy's YouTube channel. *(Link check: web access was unavailable on ingest day (2026-08-18); URLs recorded from memory were not re-verified — re-confirm before citing.)*

All initial wiki pages carry a `Source:` line pointing back to this talk. Facts and numbers in those pages are "as stated in the talk" and reflect a late-2023 snapshot of the field.

## Papers referenced by the talk's concepts

- Vaswani et al., *Attention Is All You Need* (2017) — the Transformer architecture. arXiv:1706.03762
- Brown et al., *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners* (GPT-3, 2020) — the emergent few-shot result. arXiv:2005.14165
- Ouyang et al., *Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback* (InstructGPT, 2022) — the SFT + RLHF recipe behind assistants. arXiv:2203.02155
- OpenAI, *GPT-4 Technical Report* (2023) — size undisclosed; the "rumored ~1T" note in this wiki is speculation, not from the report. arXiv:2303.08774

## Further learning (Karpathy, same lineage)

- *Neural Networks: Zero to Hero* — video course building neural nets from scratch, up to GPT.
- *Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out* (nanoGPT video) — the hands-on counterpart to [What is an LLM?](what-is-an-llm.md).
- *Let's build the GPT Tokenizer* — companion deep-dive on tokens.
