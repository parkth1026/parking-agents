# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator, formerly Director of AI at Tesla and founding member
of OpenAI. Known for making deep learning concepts accessible through hands-on
lectures and open-source projects.

## Key Facts
- PhD from Stanford under Fei-Fei Li (ImageNet, computer vision)
- Co-founded OpenAI (2015)
- Director of AI at Tesla (2017-2022), led Autopilot perception
- Returned to OpenAI (2023), now independent educator
- Created: micrograd, nanoGPT, llm.c
- Popular lecture series: "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero"

## Significance
Karpathy bridges cutting-edge research and practical understanding. His
"Intro to Large Language Models" lecture is a widely-cited single-hour overview
of the entire LLM pipeline — from pretraining to deployment to safety concerns.
His "LLM OS" framing shaped how practitioners think about LLMs as an operating
system kernel rather than just a chat interface.
