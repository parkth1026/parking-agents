# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; speaker of [[Intro to Large Language Models]], the talk that seeded this wiki.

## Key Facts

- Founding member of OpenAI (2015); later Director of AI at Tesla, leading the Autopilot vision team
- Returned to OpenAI (2023-2024); went on to found Eureka Labs (2024), an AI + education company
- Co-creator of Stanford CS231n; creator of the "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" YouTube series and of minimal teaching codebases such as nanoGPT and micrograd
- In the 2023 intro talk he popularized compact mental models of the field: the two-file view of a [[Large Language Model]], the two-stage training story, and the [[LLM OS]] vision

## Significance

One of the most-watched explainers of LLM fundamentals. His framing of [[Pretraining]] versus [[Fine-tuning]], and his warnings about [[Jailbreaking]] and [[Prompt Injection]], shape how practitioners talk about models.
