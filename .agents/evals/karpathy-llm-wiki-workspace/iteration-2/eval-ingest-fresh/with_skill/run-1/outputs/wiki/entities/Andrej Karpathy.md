# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; founding member of OpenAI (2015), former Director
of AI at Tesla leading Autopilot vision (2017-2022), and later known for
from-scratch teaching projects (Stanford CS231n, "Neural Networks: Zero to
Hero", nanoGPT, llama2.c).

## Key Facts

- Founding member of OpenAI (2015); returned to OpenAI in 2023.
- Signature teaching style: build the whole system from scratch until it is
  demystified — an LLM is "just" a parameters file plus a runner program.
- In the [[Karpathy Intro to LLMs Talk]] he framed the field's trajectory as
  the [[LLM OS]]: models as kernels with tools as peripherals.

## Significance

One of the most effective explainers of the LLM stack. His talks and courses
popularized the mental model of [[Pretraining]] + [[Fine-Tuning]] + [[RLHF]]
that this wiki is built on, plus the [[Next Token Prediction]] framing of
both what [[Large Language Model]] systems can do and how they fail.
